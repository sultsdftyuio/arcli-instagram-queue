Zapier's first customers came from other companies' help forums.

Co-founder Wade Foster spent hours reading them, looking for people who were asking for two apps to work together. He answered each one himself, with a link to a demo. By his account, each reply brought 10 to 20 visitors, and about half said yes.

He went where people were already asking.
